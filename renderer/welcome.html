Drippy

The transparency layer for your AI use. A small capsule tucked into the corner, never in the way: clicks pass straight through everything except him.

How to read the capsule

Tucked tabquiet. No AI running on this Mac
Cyan glowAI energy flowing right now, yours or a background agent's; both are metered
Violetcaution: something mildly sensitive (a phone number, a date of birth) is in your clipboard or composer
Swells + "!"critical: a secret (an API key, card number, password) is about to leave this Mac. The only time Drippy takes up space uninvited. Hover for what he found and the one-click remedy; click to acknowledge
Hoverthe capsule opens into a bar with today's numbers: energy, water and measured spend (the 💧 menu has the full breakdown and equivalents)
Clickyour 30-day trends
Notice cardsoccasional cards above him when the meters show something worth knowing. Each says why it fired and its button does the thing; they all collect in 💧 → Notices

What Drippy can see, and what it can't

  • It watches traffic patterns to Anthropic (timing and volume). It does not decrypt or read your conversations.
  • It knows that you're typing (system idle timer), never what you type, except the optional privacy scan below, which checks text on-device and instantly discards it. Only the category ("email address") is kept.
  • While you're using Claude, it checks your clipboard for secrets (an API key, a password, a .env line) so it can warn you before you paste one in, even if you copied it from a browser. Checked on-device, content discarded, only the category kept. It stops when you're not using Claude.
  • Everything stays on this Mac. Drippy has no account, no cloud, no analytics, and its only network activity is looking up Anthropic's addresses.

One optional permission

To warn you while you type sensitive data into Claude, Drippy needs macOS Accessibility access. Without it, the clipboard guard still works.
