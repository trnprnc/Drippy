Four measures, watched honestly

Drippy is pro-transparent-AI, not anti-AI. He never reads your conversations and never phones home; he watches patterns, on-device, and tells only you.

AI usage

Who is using AI on this Mac right now, you or a background agent, and how much. Claude Code usage is exact, read from its own local logs.

Environment

Energy, water and carbon per day, honest about uncertainty (±3× where estimated), every factor cited and versioned.

Spend

Your measured usage priced at Anthropic's current API list rates, cache-aware. Estimates are never priced; no false precision.

Privacy

An API key on your clipboard, a card number in your composer: Drippy turns violet before it leaves your Mac, and tells you what to do about it.

Always present, never in the way

Measured, not guessed

Claude Code reports its exact token counts, so that part of your day is measured: real tokens priced at Anthropic's current list rates, cache reads and writes included. Network-observed traffic stays an honest estimate with its ±3× band shown. Drippy never blends the two.

Spend at API rates. What your measured usage would cost if paid per token: the number your subscription hides.

Notices, grounded in the meters

When the numbers show something worth knowing, a small card appears above the droplet: a big model doing small jobs, an agent drawing energy solo for half an hour, a novel of context re-sent every turn. Every notice says why it fired ("because: 14 requests today, mostly short answers") and its button does the thing: copies the lighter-usage remedy or opens the right window. No sound, no stolen focus, all reviewable later.

Install in three steps

  1. Download and unzip, then drag Drippy.app into Applications.
  2. First launch: this test build is not yet notarised, so macOS will refuse it once. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. (On older macOS: right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again.)
  3. Say hello. Drippy appears bottom-right and gives you his tour. Drag him wherever you like; wherever you drop him becomes home. He lives in the menu bar as 💧 and starts at login.

Optional: to be warned while you type sensitive data into Claude, grant Accessibility when the welcome sheet offers it. Everything else works without it.
