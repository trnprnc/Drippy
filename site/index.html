Three things, watched honestly

Drippy is pro-transparent-AI, not anti-AI. He never reads your conversations and never phones home; he watches patterns, on-device, and tells only you.

AI usage

Environment

Privacy

An API key on your clipboard, a card number in your composer: Drippy turns violet before it leaves your Mac, and tells you what to do about it.

And sometimes, he becomes things

Always for a reason. Drippy morphs his whole body to make a point, then pops back to being a blob.

The bicycle arrives with a wellbeing nudge: you have been at it a while, go stretch your legs. Note who is riding.

The magnifying glass means he has had a close look at your writing: those are his own eyes, magnified, checking the AI tells before you hit send.

A voice of reason while you work

Drippy makes small, practical suggestions in a bubble above him, and every card earns its place: it says why it fired ("because: 9 requests in the last 30 minutes") and its button does the thing: copies the sharper prompt, starts a real five-minute break, shows exactly which AI tells he spotted. No sound, no stolen focus, and every suggestion is kept in a reviewable feed. He guides your practice; he never does the work.

Install in three steps

  1. Download and unzip, then drag Drippy.app into Applications.
  2. First launch: this test build is not yet notarised, so macOS will refuse it once. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. (On older macOS: right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again.)
  3. Say hello. Drippy appears bottom-right and gives you his tour. Drag him wherever you like; wherever you drop him becomes home. He lives in the menu bar as 💧 and starts at login.

Optional: to be warned while you type sensitive data into Claude, grant Accessibility when the welcome sheet offers it. Everything else works without it.
