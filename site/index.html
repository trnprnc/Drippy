Install in three steps

  1. Download and unzip, then drag Drippy.app into Applications.
  2. Clear the download flag once. This build is not yet notarised, so macOS blocks it. Open Terminal and paste:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Drippy.app
    Then open Drippy normally. (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway works on some macOS versions too, but the command is reliable.)
  3. Say hello. Drippy slides into your menu bar and gives you a quick tour. He starts with you at login and settles wherever you drag him along the bar.

No permissions to grant: Drippy needs no Accessibility or Automation access.
